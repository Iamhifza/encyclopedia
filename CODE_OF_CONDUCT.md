# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

* Assuming good faith, particularly in disagreements about terminology
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Citing evidence rather than asserting authority
* Being willing to write "this is contested" instead of arguing for a winner
* Focusing on what is best for readers who are encountering a term for the first
  time

Unacceptable behaviour:

* Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
* Publishing others' private information without permission
* Sustained disruption of discussions
* Personal or political attacks
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## A note specific to this project

Much of this encyclopedia documents terminology that people genuinely disagree
about. Arguing that a definition is wrong is welcome and useful. Arguing that
someone is stupid for holding it is not. When a dispute is real, the correct
outcome is usually an entry that documents the disagreement -- not a winner.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take appropriate
corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate. They have the
right to remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of
Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainers via a GitHub issue marked confidential, or by the
contact address listed in the repository profile. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly.
